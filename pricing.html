This is everything Mosim charges. If your trip is shorter or longer, we prorate the fee to your actual number of days — so you only pay for the days we're by your side.

Paid directly — no markup

Your care, hotel, and flights — paid straight to them.

Three things are not part of our fee: your medical care, your hotel, and your flights. We arrange every one of them — but you're billed directly by the hospital, the hotel, and the airline. Mosim never collects that money, never adds a markup, and never takes a cut. You always see the real costs, exactly as they are.
