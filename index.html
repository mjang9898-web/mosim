Honest answers

Questions our
guests usually ask.

Short and direct — the way a good concierge would explain it over coffee.

Pricing

Are the prices I see on this site what I'll pay?

No — those are baselines from our partner hospitals. You pay each clinic directly at service. Our flat concierge fee is separate, with no commissions and no markups.

When do I pay, and to whom?

A refundable deposit holds your itinerary. After that: hospitals at point of service, hotels at check-out, our concierge fee at booking. One transparent invoice — no surprises.

Safety & Trust

Are the hospitals you partner with safe?

JCI-accredited tertiary academic centers — Asan, Samsung Medical, Severance, Seoul St. Mary's, SNUH. The same hospitals Korean presidents use.

Will the doctors speak English?

Yes — every partner runs an International Medical Center with English-speaking staff. Your medical concierge sits in on consultations to bridge any nuance.

What if something goes wrong?

Every itinerary is pre-cleared for medical fit before you fly. Complications during your stay get same-day specialist care at no markup. Discharge plans come in English for your US physician.

Logistics

How long should we plan to stay?

Screening + cultural week: 5–7 days. Surgery or stem-cell protocols: 10–14 days for recovery. We calibrate to your schedule.

Do we need a visa?

US citizens get 90 days visa-free. The only paperwork is the K-ETA electronic authorization — we help you file.

Can my spouse come along?

Yes — the funnel is built around couples. Companions get a parallel cultural itinerary while you're in clinic, and recovery suites accommodate two.

Insurance

Will my US insurance cover anything I do in Korea?

Usually no — most US plans exclude elective and overseas care. HSAs sometimes qualify; we'll point you to a US brokerage if needed. Most clients pay out of pocket and still pay a fraction of US pricing — even with our fee.

Why Korea & aftercare

Why Korea and not Thailand or Mexico?

Korea ranks #1 worldwide in plastic surgery, dental, and ophthalmology — and is the only OECD country with approved commercial stem-cell therapy. Care happens in academic hospitals, not standalone clinics.

What about follow-up care once we're home?

English discharge plan, all imaging on a portable drive, and a 30-day concierge for prescription refills, telemedicine, or US-doctor coordination.
